About the position
  • Pay: $25 to $30 an hour plus commission. Late nights come with premium pay.
  • Sign-on bonus: $1,000.
    Sign-on bonus terms

    The $1,000 sign-on bonus is paid through payroll in three parts: $100 after 30 days of employment, $300 after 90 days and $600 after 6 months, counted from your first day. Each part is earned only if you are still employed on that date. If your employment ends before a date for any reason, including if you are terminated, resign or quit, no later part is owed. Parts already paid are yours to keep. Normal tax withholding applies.

  • Paid training: learn next to a top host before you go live.
  • Your hours: part-time or full-time, chosen from open shifts.
  • All hosts are W-2 employees.
  • Role: live sales host. You go live to buyers across the country and sell on camera. Selling is the job: you move shoes, one after another, and turn viewers into buyers.
  • On a show: keep the chat fun and fast, answer comments on the spot, and make every show worth watching. A crew of two backs you up so you can focus on entertaining the audience and closing the sale.
  • Open shifts: mornings, early afternoons (before 3 PM), late nights (after 11:30 PM, premium pay) and weekends.
  • You need to be 18 or older. Our studio is in Jefferson Park, near the Blue Line and Metra.
  • Bonus and commission details are shared in your offer.
